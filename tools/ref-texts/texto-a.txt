The mixture-of-experts layer routes each token to a small subset of feed-forward networks. During decoding, only the selected experts are read from memory, so the cost of a token depends on where those experts live: in GPU memory, they are read at hundreds of gigabytes per second; in host memory, they must either be copied across the PCIe bus or computed on the CPU.

def top_k(scores, k):
    order = sorted(range(len(scores)), key=lambda i: scores[i], reverse=True)
    return order[:k]

O roteador escolhe oito especialistas por token. Quando o mesmo especialista aparece em tokens seguidos, mantê-lo na memória da placa evita uma nova cópia. A política de colocação decide quais especialistas ficam residentes, usando o custo medido de cada falta em vez de apenas contar acessos.

Question: if a cache miss costs twenty-eight times more than a hit, how much does a one percent improvement in hit rate save per token?
Answer: it depends on how many experts are read per token and on whether the copy overlaps with computation.
